THE HYBRID CORE — HOW TO TEST ON YOUR PC
=========================================

Turn your speakers/headphones DOWN first. Close any app that is playing audio
(DAW, browser, Suno): The Hybrid Core takes exclusive control of the device.

1) Desktop: "The Hybrid Core"  (the main app)
   - The console opens on your Main-Faceplate art: 13 stems in the big panel,
     Main / Aux 1-3 buses in the left panel.
   - Glass display (top right): song position / length, DSP load %, driver, buffer,
     latency in ms, dropouts.
   - Round buttons, left to right: LOAD (pick your stems), START (back to the top),
     PLAY / PAUSE, STOP (stops and goes back to the top), LOOP (blue = loops the song),
     SOURCE (cycles TONES -> INPUTS -> STEMS).
   - Top bar: OPEN / SAVE / EXPORT (also Ctrl+O, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Shift+S = save as, Ctrl+E).
       SAVE    keeps the song as a .hcs session file: which stems are loaded, every knob, fader
               and button, loop, and where the song was. Keep it in the song's folder: if you
               move or copy the whole folder, it still finds the stems.
       OPEN    loads a session back (stems reload in the background). Double-clicking a .hcs
               file in Explorer opens it too (if the console is already open, it opens there).
       EXPORT  renders the mix to a WAV file (48 kHz, 24-bit) exactly as long as the song,
               with everything you set: EQ, compressors, faders, mutes, limiter. It runs in the
               background (the button shows %); press EXPORT again to cancel. The music keeps
               playing while it exports. The window title shows the session name.
   - Device: click the device name panel (top left) for the next sound device,
     right-click it for the previous one.
   - Bottom bar: the whole song as a waveform. Click or drag on it to jump there.
   - Keys: Space = play/pause, Home = start, End = stop, L = loop on/off,
     Left/Right arrow = 5 s back/forward (hold Ctrl: 30 s).
   - Right-click any S button = clear every solo; right-click any stem M = clear every mute.
   - YOUR STEMS: click LOAD and pick up to 13 WAV / MP3 / FLAC files (they go in name order:
     name them 01 Kick, 02 Snare... to control the order). Or drag files or a whole folder from
     Explorer onto the console: dropped on a strip they start at that strip, elsewhere at the
     first empty strip. Each strip shows LOADING %, then the file name, format and length.
     Anything not 48 kHz is converted to 48 kHz while it loads. Loading never starts playback:
     press PLAY. All stems play together; the song is as long as the longest stem.
     Right-click a strip's name to empty it. Your stems come back next time you open the app.
   - Tabs: MIX / EQ / DYNAMICS change what the 5 knobs on every stem do:
       MIX      = TRIM, PAN, A1 / A2 / A3 sends
       EQ       = LOW (shelf 100 Hz), HIGH (shelf 10 kHz), CUT (low cut, all the way left = off),
                  MID (boost / cut), FREQ (where the MID works, 150 Hz - 8 kHz)
       DYNAMICS = compressor: THRESH, RATIO (all the way left = off), ATK, REL, GAIN (makeup)
     An orange bar hanging from the top of a stem meter = how much the compressor is turning it down.
     FADERS tab: click to switch the stem faders between MAIN, AUX 1, AUX 2, AUX 3 sends.
   - LIM (bottom bar, blue = on) is a safety limiter on the main output: nothing goes over
     -0.3 dB. GR next to it shows how much it is turning down. It adds 1 ms of delay.
   - Knobs: drag up/down. Hold Shift for fine moves. Double-click = reset. Mouse wheel works too.
   - Faders: drag the cap. M = mute, S = solo, Ø = phase invert.
   - The last device in the list is "OFFLINE CLOCK - no sound" (meters run, nothing plays).
   - Your settings are saved automatically and come back next time.
   >> Screenshot the window and send it to Claude.

   A different faceplate: save it as Main-Faceplate.png (same layout, 16:9, 4K or 8K) in
   %APPDATA%\HybridCore\skins\ and restart. It is scaled to your window before it reaches
   the graphics card.

2) Start menu > The Hybrid Core > Diagnostics > "1 - Rig Check"
   Checks your CPU, lists your sound devices and ASIO drivers, plays 2 seconds of
   SILENCE through each one and measures the real buffer size and timing.
   Last line should say: RIG READY.   >> Screenshot it.

3) Diagnostics > "2 - Mixer Test (WASAPI)" and "3 - Mixer Test (ASIO)"
   15 seconds each. After 5 s only ONE low note (solo); after 10 s the chord returns
   with that note in the LEFT ear. Last line: STREAM OK.   >> Screenshot them.

4) Diagnostics > "4 - Engine Self-Test" — runs the built-in tests (no sound).
   Last line should say: ... 0 failed -> PASS

5) Diagnostics > "5 - Screen Snapshots" — renders the console on YOUR graphics card at
   3840x2160 and 1389x868 into a "HybridCore Snapshots" folder on your Desktop, next to your
   faceplate, and prints frame times.   >> Send the folder's PNGs and a screenshot of the text.

6) VST3 (optional): in your DAW, rescan plug-ins and look for "The Hybrid Core"
   (vendor Hybrid Elite Mafia). It has 13 stereo inputs (Stem 1 + 12 side-chain inputs)
   and 4 stereo outputs. It uses the DAW's own generic controls (no custom screen yet).

If something says "open failed": another app is using the device, or the device does
not allow exclusive mode. Screenshot that message too.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" on install because the installer is not
code-signed. Click "More info" then "Run anyway".
