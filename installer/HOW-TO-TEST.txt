THE HYBRID CORE — HOW TO TEST ON YOUR PC
=========================================

Turn your speakers/headphones DOWN first. Close any app that is playing audio
(DAW, browser, Suno): The Hybrid Core takes exclusive control of the device.

1) Desktop: "The Hybrid Core"  (the main app)
   - The console opens: 13 stem strips + Main / Aux 1-3 buses.
   - Top bar shows the audio device, buffer size in ms and live CPU %.
   - SOURCE = TEST TONES by default: you hear a soft chord and the meters move.
   - Click DEVICE to step through your outputs (WASAPI) and ASIO drivers.
   - Click THEME to switch Pearl White / Obsidian / Titanium.
   - Knobs: drag up/down. Hold Shift for fine moves. Double-click = reset. Mouse wheel works too.
   - Faders: drag the cap. M = mute, S = solo, Ø = phase invert.
   - Your settings are saved automatically and come back next time.
   >> Screenshot the window and send it to Claude.

   Your own faceplate art: put pearl.png / obsidian.png / titanium.png (any size, 8K is
   fine) into %APPDATA%\HybridCore\skins\ and restart. It is scaled to your screen and
   compressed before it reaches the graphics card.

2) Start menu > The Hybrid Core > Diagnostics > "1 - Rig Check"
   Checks your CPU, lists your sound devices and ASIO drivers, plays 2 seconds of
   SILENCE through each one and measures the real buffer size and timing.
   Last line should say: RIG READY.   >> Screenshot it.

3) Diagnostics > "2 - Mixer Test (WASAPI)" and "3 - Mixer Test (ASIO)"
   15 seconds each. After 5 s only ONE low note (solo); after 10 s the chord returns
   with that note in the LEFT ear. Last line: STREAM OK.   >> Screenshot them.

4) Diagnostics > "4 - Engine Self-Test" — runs the built-in tests (no sound).
   Last line should say: ... 0 failed -> PASS

5) VST3 (optional): in your DAW, rescan plug-ins and look for "The Hybrid Core"
   (vendor Hybrid Elite Mafia). It has 13 stereo inputs (Stem 1 + 12 side-chain inputs)
   and 4 stereo outputs. It uses the DAW's own generic controls (no custom screen yet).

If something says "open failed": another app is using the device, or the device does
not allow exclusive mode. Screenshot that message too.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" on install because the installer is not
code-signed. Click "More info" then "Run anyway".
