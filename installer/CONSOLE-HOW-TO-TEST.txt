HEM DIGITAL CONSOLE - HOW TO TEST
=================================

1. START IT
   Double-click "HEM Digital Console" on the desktop. It opens full screen.
   The DEVICE button (bottom right) shows where the sound goes. Click it to step to the next output
   (your speakers, the soundbar on HDMI, your headset). If an output can't play, it says so - click DEVICE again.

2. LOAD YOUR STEMS
   Click LOAD STEMS (bottom left) or OPEN and pick the stem files (WAV, MP3, FLAC) - or drag them onto the desk.
   Suno stems go to their own channels: Vocals 1, Backing Vocals 2, Drums 3, Percussion 4, Bass 5, Guitar 6,
   Keyboard 7, Synth 8, Strings 9, Brass 10, Woodwinds 11, FX 12. Other names fill the free channels in name order.
   The whole-song file Suno puts in the folder ("0 <song>.wav") or a master goes to EXT 1 (a reference: press
   MONITOR SOURCE EXT 1 to hear it; it is not in the mix). More than 13 files: it tells you which were left out.
   Drop ONE file onto a channel strip to load just that channel.
   Click a channel's name strip (bottom of the channel) to load one file into just that channel.
   Right-click a name strip to empty it.

3. PLAY
   PLAY / PAUSE, STOP, |< (back to the start), LOOP. Click or drag the long bar to jump in the song.
   Keyboard: SPACE play/pause, HOME start, END stop, L loop, 1-7 the pages, F11 full screen (ESC leaves it).

4. THE PAGES (tabs at the top)
   DESK       the 13-channel desk: TRIM, MONO, PHASE, HPF, two cue sends, 3-band EQ with the two BELL switches,
              the one-knob compressor, PAN, INSERT, BUS B, PFL, faders and meters. Right side: bus compressor,
              EXT returns, Bus B, the monitor section, the main meter and the main fader.
   BIG DESK   the same desk, 5 channels at a time with bigger knobs. BACK / NEXT, the numbers 1-13, the Left / Right
              arrow keys or the mouse wheel on bare plate move across all 13 channels.
   STEP EQ    the 2-channel 4-band stepped mastering EQ. MODEL picks STANDARD or SPECIAL EDITION (21 steps on every
              band, 20 Hz and 40 kHz bands, HP-FILT + HPF knob, M/S: channel 1 = mid, channel 2 = side).
   TWIN BUS   the 2-channel bus compressor with the dynamic EQ.
   COLOUR     drive, tone EQ, HF compressor, stereo image, transformer.
   METER      the loudness meter: RUN (pause / go on), RESET, VIEW (RADAR, RTA spectrum, vector SCOPE), AUTO (RTA),
              PEAK and LOUD lamps, the loudness numbers, the peak meter, correlation and mono deviation. EDIT on the
              right: RADAR, RTA, SCOPE, GENERAL (layout, units, alerts, meter scale) and LIBRARY (presets: RECALL,
              STORE into USER 1-8). FOLLOW PLAY measures only while playing; RESET ON PLAY starts again on PLAY.
   TOOLS      the front-end tools that sit where the desk has its mic preamps: RESO TAMER (two dynamic notches),
              SHUFFLE (mud and width), TAP ECHO (delay with TAP). Pick a tool on a channel with its TOOL window
              (top of the channel), then light that channel's INS switch. ED opens it on the TOOLS page.

5. THE RACK
   The STEP EQ, TWIN BUS and COLOUR STAGE sit on the desk's MAIN INSERT. Light MAIN INSERT (desk, right side)
   to hear them. RACK at the top picks their order. RACK IN (desk) or RACK IN on each page takes a unit out.

5b. SCREEN
   The whole console always fits the window - no scrolling, every button on screen. Want it bigger? The BIG DESK tab.
   The window opens where you left it (size, place, maximized). F11 = full screen, ESC leaves it.

6. KNOBS
   Drag up / down. Hold SHIFT or CTRL for fine moves. Mouse wheel works too (SHIFT + wheel = fine).
   Double-click (or right-click) a knob or fader: back to its start position.
   Hover any control to see its name and value.

7. SAVE, OPEN, EXPORT
   SAVE (Ctrl+S): the first time it asks for a name (starts in Downloads); after that it saves in place and the "*"
   in the title bar goes away. SAVE AS (Ctrl+Shift+S): a new name. OPEN (Ctrl+O): a session, or audio files as stems.
   RECENT: your last 10 sessions. NEW (Ctrl+N) starts clean. Before NEW, OPEN or closing with unsaved changes it asks
   "Save changes?". Saving is safe (written beside the old file, then swapped in). A recovery copy is kept every
   3 minutes; if the console ever closes by surprise, the next start offers it. Stems moved to another folder:
   it asks where they are and finds them by name.
   EXPORT writes the MAIN OUT to a WAV (24-bit, 48 kHz), offline, with the same sound as playback.
   PRINT STEMS writes each channel's direct out to its own WAV (D.POST on a channel = after its fader).
   When you close the console, everything is kept and comes back the next time you open it.

8. RESET
   RESET (bottom bar) asks SURE?, then puts every control back to its start (your stems stay).
   For 15 seconds after, it says UNDO: press it to get everything back.

9. DIAGNOSTICS (Start menu, HEM Digital Console folder)
   Rig Check         lists your CPU and audio outputs.
   Engine Self-Test  runs every measured test of the engines (the same tests GitHub runs).
   Screen Snapshots  saves pictures of every page to a folder on your desktop.

WHAT IS NOT KNOWN YET
   The sound is built from the manuals and the makers' published graphs, "in the spirit of" the real gear.
   It has not been compared by ear or by recording against your engineer's actual units.
   Guesses are labelled ASSUMED in the code (one file: ConsoleSpecs.hpp).
